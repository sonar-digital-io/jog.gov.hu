Imprint

The National Legislation Database, as well as a legal information search tool supported by artificial intelligence and based on the content of that Database, European Union legal acts, and in-depth analysis of court decisions (hereinafter “AI Search Tool”) are operated as digital public services by MKIFK Magyar Közlönykiadó és Igazságügyi Fordítóközpont Zártkörűen Működő Részvénytársaság (MKIFK Zrt) to ensure broad accessibility to and comprehension of legislation and support access to justice. The National Legislation Database, as a digital public service, is an electronic collection of legislation containing consolidated texts of laws.

The National Legislation Database and the AI Search Tool are provided by MKIFK Zrt. to the public free of charge, via the internet.

The operation of the National Legislation Database is governed by Act CXXX of 2010 on law-making, with detailed regulations provided in Government Decree 338/2011 (29 December) on the National Legislation Database. The rules governing the operation of the AI Search Tool are set out in Government Decree 338/2011 (29 December) on the National Legislation Database.

Publisher: Ministry of Justice
