3.            Description of the Service

The services of the Portal (NJT, AI Search Tool) are provided by MKIFK Zrt. to the public free of charge, online.

3.1.        National Legislation Database

The NJT is an electronic collection of legislation containing consolidated texts of laws, based on Section 29 (1) of Act CXXX of 2010 on law-making, with the content and on the website specified in Government Decree 338/2011 (29 December) on the National Legislation Database (hereinafter: “Decree”), operated as a digital public service accessible free of charge to all. The National Legislation Database is a legislation search service available to all, free of charge.

The NJT is available at the following service levels:

•             a service with content pursuant to Section 2(1) and (2) of the Decree, for users without identification,

•             legislation database service with an extended scope subject to identification; the related content and query service; as well as other convenience functions,

•             a service level available through the Integrated Legislation System (hereinafter “IJR”) to users identified with a token allocated by the IJR authentication module, providing comprehensive access to all NJT content and query services, as well as specific functions related to the IJR.

3.2 Artificial Intelligence-Supported Legal Information Search Tool

The AI Search Tool helps users by providing textual summaries to find and understand laws related to their life situations.

The service operates based on the legislative texts in the National Legislation Database, as follows:

•             the user asks a question in everyday language on the system's web interface,

•             the question is processed through linguistic and semantic interpretation, and then

•             the relevant laws are listed based on vector similarity search.

The purpose of the solution is to provide results from laws for questions formulated in everyday language. This does not constitute legal advice but, by supplying information, helps legally unqualified citizens.

The added value of the system is provided by artificial intelligence technology that allows for semantic search. This means that the system not only searches for keywords but can also interpret the meaning of the question formulated by the user. The relevant legal documents are displayed accordingly. In contrast, conventional search systems look for only keywords, as well as their inflected forms or synonyms, in the texts.

The advantage of the semantic search tool is that it allows users to ask questions in full sentences, using everyday language, similarly to a conversation with a legal advisor. There is no need to know precise legal terminology; the system is capable of interpreting colloquial expressions as well.

A special feature of the system is that it can also provide a textual summary in response to users’ questions based on the laws and other relevant documents found, with references to the applicable legal provisions.

The search tool reviews not only legislation currently in force, but also versions of laws relevant at earlier or subsequent points in time compared to the date set by the user.

The artificial intelligence-supported legal information search system offers the following key services:

•             Semantic search within the full scope of currently effective Hungarian legislation, which is capable of interpreting questions formulated in everyday language, and displays relevant documents accordingly.

•             Ranking based on relevance, where the system arranges the documents found according to their importance and how they are related. The results list provides text excerpts and direct links to the full legal texts (with NJT links).

•             Providing a textual summary response to users’ questions. The answers are generated based on the contents of the laws found, and include precise references to the relevant legal provisions.

•             User feedback can be provided, which contributes to continuous system development, improved accuracy, and optimised user experience.

The system's main search interface helps users in various ways to find legal information quickly and efficiently. The user interface design is simple and clear, supporting questions formulated in everyday language, as well as conventional keyword search. Key elements of the search interface:

•             Search field: This is where keywords, questions in everyday language, or specific references to laws (number, title, abridged name of a law, as well as specific legal provisions) can be typed.

•             Search start button: Starting the search based on the specified terms or questions.

•             Calendar for selecting the date, ensuring that the tool can also search for laws not currently in effect.

The AI Professional Search Tool displays the following items related to the question: the relevant list of laws, local government decrees, official notifications, collection of statements of reason, legal glossary, and court decisions.

The anonymised court decisions displayed by the AI Professional Search Tool are available based on Section 76 (12a) of Act CLXI of 2011 on the organisation and administration of courts. Decisions by regional courts and courts of appeal are accessible with the appropriate NJT access rights.
